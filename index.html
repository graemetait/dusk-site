Dusk

Dark Mode for Safari

Dusk adds dark mode to every website in Safari, while preserving images, videos, and other media in their original colours.

  • Follows macOS light/dark appearance automatically, or override manually
  • Auto-detection skips sites that already have a dark theme
  • Per-site control for individual domains
  • Adjustable brightness and contrast

Support

If you have questions, feedback, or run into an issue, get in touch at duskdarkmode@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Dusk does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The extension runs entirely on your Mac.

Dusk uses browser.storage.sync to save your preferences (enabled/disabled state, per-site settings, brightness, and contrast). This data stays on your device and is not sent to any server.

Dusk has no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services.
